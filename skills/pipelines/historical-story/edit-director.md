# Historical Story Edit Director

Assemble the edit around locked narration timing.

Keep every cut aligned to `narration_timing`; do not stretch voice to solve visual gaps. Preserve factual qualifiers and labels for archival, illustrative, and dramatic reconstruction treatment.

For the full-longform visual-board contract, one final board may span 20–30 seconds (up to 36 seconds with an explicit hold reason). Convert its 3–5 internal microbeats into deterministic motion decisions: slow push/pan, crop changes, map-route drawing, evidence-layer reveals, captions, reconstruction labels, and audio ducking. These are edit decisions executed by the selected composition runtime; they are not a second post-render effect pass.

Audit rhythm: no long run of identical shot sizes, no repeated primary final image across boards, no silent filler, music ducked beneath narration, and every act has a clear arrival and landing. A board may reuse a core map or diagram as a supporting overlay, but its primary contextual image must remain unique.

Output `edit_decisions` and stop for human approval.
