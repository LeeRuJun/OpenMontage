# Historical Story Intake Director

Turn a historical topic into a bounded story brief.

## Required decisions

- historical subject and scope
- central story question
- narrative subject: person, group, institution, place, or event
- point of view and audience
- `production_mode`: `pilot_2min` or `full_longform`
- target canvas/platform and disclosure policy

## Duration contract

Pilot: 90–150 seconds, target 120. Full: 1,320–1,500 measured narration seconds. Never promise long-form duration without a narration plan.

## Output

Write `brief` with `metadata.historical_story` containing `mode`, `story_question`, `point_of_view`, `fact_boundary`, `duration_contract`, `visual_treatment`, and `disclosure_policy`.
