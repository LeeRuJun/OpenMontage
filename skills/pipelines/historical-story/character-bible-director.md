# Historical Character Bible Director

Design stable, source-backed character references for a narrated historical explainer.

The character image is a visual identity anchor, not a character-animation rig and not automatically a final shot. Record identity, evidence source IDs, period markers, appearance anchors, costume/material details, props, age/period variant, and continuity rules.

Do not require detailed acting, choreography, gaze paths, dialogue blocking, or frame-by-frame character motion. The narration and editorial graphics carry the explanation; the character reference only identifies the historical subject and can be placed inside a context scene or evidence layout.

Reject unsupported likenesses, invented meetings, anachronistic objects, false uniforms, fabricated documents, and unlabelled dramatic reconstructions.

Output `historical_character_bible` and stop for review before core asset generation.
