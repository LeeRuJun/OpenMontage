# Script Director

Write an original Chinese deep explanation, not a book summary or chapter-by-chapter transcription. The video must make the
book's ideas understandable through a central tension, concrete scenes, reasoning, counterexamples, and the narrator's own
judgment. Use hook → lived problem → idea in action → why it works → where it fails → what to try → callback. For each
section record source IDs, quote markers, estimated seconds, and visual intent.
Keep narration within 10% of the approved duration and include a distinct viewer takeaway per chapter.
Write for natural speech: varied sentence length, conversational transitions, purposeful pauses, and one clear story turn per section.
Do not enumerate chapters or recite takeaways. Every section must answer a viewer question or change how the viewer sees a problem.
Style profile: knowledge-led but conversational; start from a daily observation, translate the idea through a story or analogy,
add a clear personal judgment, and land on a concrete choice. Do not imitate any named living presenter or reuse signature catchphrases.
