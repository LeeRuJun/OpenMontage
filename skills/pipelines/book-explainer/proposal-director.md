# Proposal Director

Turn research into three genuinely different viewer-facing deep-explanation concepts. Select one narrative mode based on the book;
the chosen concept must be a question, tension, or transformation rather than a summary of the table of contents.
Set a hook, payoff, chapter count, estimated duration from 18-25 minutes, and a support-image budget of 20-35.
Make narration and script the primary experience; present voice performance, natural pacing, subtle music, A-roll/B-roll ratio,
render runtime, cost, and the no-AI-video default before approval.
